# Modules

## Patient & Clinical
Registration, patient timeline, encounters, allergies, diagnoses, orders, notes, care plans and discharge.

## Hospital Operations
Appointments, queues, admissions, transfers, bed management, nursing and emergency.

## Diagnostics
Laboratory, radiology, PACS/RIS integration and reporting.

## Medication
Pharmacy, prescriptions, dispensing and medication safety workflows.

## Finance
Billing, insurance/TPA, payments, estimates, refunds and reporting.

## Enterprise
HR, workforce, procurement, inventory, biomedical equipment, housekeeping, dietary, ambulance and facilities.

## Command Center
Capacity, patient flow, emergency load, bed status, diagnostics, pharmacy and operational alerts.
